THE SKILLSWAP IDEA

How a swap works

Four steps from sign-up to your first session.

  1. 1. List your skills

    Say what you can teach and what you want to learn.

  2. 2. Get matched

    We rank students whose skills fit yours both ways.

  3. 3. Book a session

    Message, agree a time, meet online or on campus.

  4. 4. Earn points

    Teaching earns points you spend on learning.
